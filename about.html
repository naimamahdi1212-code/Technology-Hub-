About Cascade Technologies

Technology knowledge should flow downhill — to everyone, not just the well-connected.

That's the idea the name comes from: a cascade, where insight from the top of the field reaches every terrace below it.

Our mission

Make world-class technical knowledge and community genuinely reachable.

Cascade Technologies exists to close the gap between where technology is moving and where most people are able to learn about it. We bring technical education, research, discussion, and networking into a single platform — built by practitioners, for practitioners.

Our vision

A global terrace of builders, each one lifting the next.

We want Cascade to be the place a self-taught developer, a PhD researcher, and a first-time founder can all learn from each other in the same thread — and where that exchange compounds into real careers, real companies, and real research.

What we stand for

Core values

Innovation

We build for where the field is going, not where it's been — and we ship early, opinionated takes rather than safe consensus.

Trust

Every claim on Cascade is attributable to a person or a source. Reputation is earned publicly, thread by thread.

Community first

The forum isn't a feature bolted onto content — it's the core loop. Knowledge flows both directions, always.

Access

Technical depth without gatekeeping. Free to browse, free to ask, free to teach.

Our story

How Cascade came together

2021

A Slack group of 40 engineers

Cascade began as an informal channel where a handful of engineers shared what they were learning about distributed systems and early LLM tooling.

2023

The forum and Tech Hub launch

Demand for structured, searchable knowledge outpaced chat. We opened the public forum and knowledge repository the same year.

2024

Learning Center and first hackathon

Community-built courses launched alongside our first hackathon — now an annual event across six host cities.

2026

82,000+ members, 61 countries

Today Cascade is a full technology hub — news, courses, research, events, and a forum spanning ten technical categories.
